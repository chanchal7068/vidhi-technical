01 — About Us

A technical services team built on reliable execution.

Vidhi Technical Services LLC is a Dubai-based technical services and interior fit-out company specialising in gypsum works, painting, acoustic solutions and interior finishing systems. Established in May 2024, the company is backed by 18+ years of site management experience and was founded on a commitment to high-quality workmanship, professional project execution and reliable service across the UAE.

With a skilled workforce and hands-on industry experience, we provide comprehensive solutions for commercial, residential, institutional, hospitality and public-sector projects — from gypsum partitions and ceilings to specialised acoustic wall and ceiling installations.

The company currently operates with a skilled workforce of 22 team members, including supervisors, installers, and technical workers. Our team is experienced in handling site execution, material coordination, and quality finishing according to project requirements.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, professionalism, and client satisfaction. Through careful planning, skilled execution, and attention to detail, we strive to deliver projects on time and to the highest industry standards.
