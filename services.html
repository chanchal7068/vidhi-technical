01 — Gypsum Works

Gypsum Works & Drywall

We supply and install top-grade gypsum systems that form the backbone of modern commercial and residential interiors. Our focus is on structural integrity, safety, and smooth finishing.

  • Gypsum Partitions: High-quality single/double layer drywalls for office partitions and acoustics.
  • False Ceilings: Standard, fire-rated, and moisture-resistant gypsum plasterboard ceilings.
  • Bulkheads & Cove Detailing: Custom bulkheads for AC housing, light troughs, and hidden ambient lighting.

Wooden & Acoustic Panels

Specialized installation of decorative and sound-managing wall and ceiling linings, crucial for architectural acoustics and aesthetic balance.

  • Perforated Acoustic Ceilings: Micro-perforated and standard perforated MDF panels for high NRC absorption.
  • Acoustic Wall Systems: Grooved wooden acoustic panels, fabric-wrapped acoustic panels for noise reduction.
  • Auditorium and Theatres: Precision layouts matching acoustic calculation sheets and acoustic consultant guidelines.
