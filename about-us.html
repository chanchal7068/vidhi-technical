01 — Who We Are

Expertise in fit-out & interior finishes.

Vidhi Technical Services LLC is a Dubai-based technical services and interior fit-out company specializing in gypsum works, painting work, acoustic solutions, and interior finishing systems. Established in May 2024, the company is backed by 18+ years of site management experience and was founded with a commitment to delivering high-quality workmanship, professional project execution, and reliable services across the UAE.

With a skilled workforce and hands-on industry experience, we provide comprehensive solutions for commercial, residential, institutional, hospitality, and public-sector projects. Our expertise includes gypsum partitions, gypsum ceilings, decorative ceiling systems, interior fit-out works, and specialized acoustic wall and ceiling installations.

Vidhi Technical Services LLC also offers professional installation of acoustic systems for auditoriums, conference halls, multipurpose halls, theaters, educational facilities, and commercial spaces. Our capabilities include gypsum acoustic systems, perforated MDF acoustic panels, micro-perforated acoustic panels, wooden acoustic wall panels, and sound absorption solutions designed to meet both functional and aesthetic requirements.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, professionalism, and client satisfaction. Through careful planning, skilled execution, and attention to detail, we strive to deliver projects on time and to the highest industry standards.
